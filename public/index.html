A run is one transcript taken through five stages. Everything written at a stage is kept on disk in the data folder, with who confirmed what. Nothing is deleted by the app: earlier readings go to history, replaced passages to an archive, deleted runs to the trash.

How it works

Paste what you have. Take the costume off the argument, then grade what is left.

A claim, a quote, a whole transcript, or a link: paste it and go. A claim gets a search query and can be checked against the academic databases at once. A transcript is split into passages; each one is rewritten in plain words at two reading levels, with the single step where the argument jumps, the fairest defense of the speaker, what is left after it, and every claim graded on its own. Who said what is checked first when more than one person speaks. The speaker is never graded.
